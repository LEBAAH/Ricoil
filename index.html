Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our service.

Is there a cost for collection?

In many cases, collection is free depending on your volume and location. Some high-volume customers may even be eligible for rebates.

How often can you collect?

We offer flexible schedules, from weekly collections for busy restaurants to ad-hoc requests for smaller venues.

Do you provide containers?

Yes, we can provide various storage solutions, including 60L drums, 120L drums, and 1000L IBC containers.

Are you fully compliant?

Absolutely. We provide full Waste Transfer Notes (WTN) for every collection, ensuring you meet all UK legal requirements.
