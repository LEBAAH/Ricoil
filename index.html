Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our service.

How often can you collect waste oil?

We offer weekly, bi-weekly, monthly and on-demand collections to suit your business volume.

Do you provide waste transfer notes?

Yes. Every collection comes with a full waste transfer note for full compliance with UK regulations.

What areas do you cover?

We cover the entire UK mainland, from major cities to rural locations.

Is there a minimum amount required?

No minimum — we collect from small cafes producing just 20L a week to large manufacturers with 1000L+ IBC tanks.
