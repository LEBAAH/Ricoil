About RICOIL

Our mission is to power the future through sustainable waste management.

Our Story

Founded in the heart of the UK, RICOIL was born out of a desire to solve one of the most persistent problems in the catering industry: the messy and often irresponsible disposal of waste cooking oil. We saw an opportunity to not only provide a cleaner solution for businesses but also to contribute to the global transition towards renewable energy.

Today, we are one of the UK's fastest-growing waste oil collection services, partnering with thousands of establishments from independent cafes to national restaurant chains.

Our Vision

We envision a world where every drop of waste is seen as a resource. By perfecting the collection and processing of waste cooking oil, we are helping to reduce the UK's reliance on fossil fuels and protecting our environment from the damage caused by improper oil disposal in sewers and landfills.

Leading the way in sustainable waste cooking oil management across the UK.
