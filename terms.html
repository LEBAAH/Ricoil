Rules and guidelines for using our services.

1. Service Agreement

By requesting a collection from RICOIL, you agree to these terms. We provide waste cooking oil collection and recycling services for commercial businesses across the UK.

2. Oil Quality

Customers must ensure that waste cooking oil provided for collection is free from contaminants such as water, chemicals, or excessive food waste. We reserve the right to refuse collection of contaminated oil.

3. Collection Schedule

We strive to adhere to agreed collection schedules. However, schedules may be subject to change due to unforeseen circumstances or logistics. We will notify you of any major delays.

4. Compliance

RICOIL provides Waste Transfer Notes for all collections. It is the customer's responsibility to retain these documents for their records as required by UK environmental regulations.

5. Payments

Any fees or rebates related to oil collection will be agreed upon in writing. Payments or credits will be processed according to our standard billing cycles.

6. Liability

RICOIL is not liable for any indirect or consequential losses arising from our services, except where required by UK law.
