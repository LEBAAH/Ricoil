Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2024

At RICOIL Ltd ("we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following data about you:

  • Contact information (Name, email address, phone number, physical address).
  • Business details (Business name, volume of oil produced).
  • Technical data (IP address, browser type, cookies).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and manage our oil collection services.
  • Process your enquiries and provide quotes.
  • Send you operational communications (e.g., collection reminders).
  • Comply with UK environmental and waste disposal regulations.

3. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes we collected it for, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

4. Your Rights

Under the UK GDPR, you have rights including:

  • The right to access your personal data.
  • The right to rectification of inaccurate data.
  • The right to erasure of your data.
  • The right to object to processing.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at ricoil.ltd@gmail.com.
